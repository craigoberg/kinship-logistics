# Offline bus headcount (venue hop)

**Locked with the build 2026-10-16.** This is the practice note for the Policies & Procedures pack (BL-092). It is not the signed policy. Do not drop it when the transport / safeguarding procedure is written.

Help for drivers: Manifest — Start a run, and Manifest — Active run and Close Run.

## What the tablet keeps

While Manifest can reach the server, it downloads today’s venue hops and who is expected on each one:

- clients still checked in with the group
- carers travelling with those clients
- support staff and volunteers who are checked in

That download is the last known list. It is not a second manifest.

## When the group has not been released

The normal start still waits for the trip leader to release the group on Event Deliver.

If the driver must leave and that release is not on this tablet, Manifest shows **Not released to the bus yet** and **Start this leg anyway**.

1. **The expected names are on the tablet.** The driver confirms, then marks each person on the bus or not travelling. A person who was not on the list can be added by name. The office confirms that name later. The activity log is the record until then.
2. **The expected names are not on the tablet.** The driver sees: *System offline. Write who is on the bus on the paper roll for this leg and start?* The paper sheet is the headcount. It travels with the bus and is filed in the office file for this trip and this leg. Someone in the office types the names in from that sheet.

A single “all on board” tap with no names is not this procedure.

## After the signal returns

The tablet uploads in order:

- It records that the driver started the leg before Release.
- It creates or matches the one trip for that hop and applies the on-bus and not-travelling marks.
- If the trip leader releases after the bus has already left, that release joins the same leg. It does not open a second manifest.

Close Run stays locked until the queue is empty.

## Office

Look at what differed from the expected list: someone left off, someone added, or a leg whose headcount is the paper roll. Do not re-tick every person the driver already marked on the bus.

The activity log line is **Hop started before release**. A paper-roll leg says the paper is the headcount.
